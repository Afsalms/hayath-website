Materia Medica Notes  ·  01

Nux vomica

Strychnos nux-vomica

A concise study profile from classical homeopathic materia medica — exploring the traditional remedy picture, characteristic symptoms, modalities and keynotes.

Classical Materia Medica Student Reference Mind Map Included
01 / Identity

Know the source

Nux vomica is prepared from the seeds of a tropical tree known botanically as Strychnos nux-vomica.

In classical materia medica, the remedy is commonly associated with oversensitivity, irritability and characteristic digestive complaints.

Common name
Poison nut / Vomit nut
Botanical name
Strychnos nux-vomica
Family
Loganiaceae
Source
Dried ripe seeds
Origin
Tropical Asia, including India
Constituents
Strychnine and brucine
Preparation
According to applicable homoeopathic pharmacopoeial methods
02 / Remedy essence

The traditional Nux vomica picture

Classical texts commonly describe a picture of heightened sensitivity to external stimuli, irritability and digestive disturbance.

03 / General picture

Mental & physical generals

Selected themes commonly found in classical descriptions. These are materia medica notes, not personality assessments or diagnostic criteria.

Mental generals

  • Irritability and impatience
  • Easily angered by minor disturbances
  • Sensitivity to noise, light and odours
  • Difficulty tolerating interruption or contradiction
  • Driven or work-oriented tendencies in some classical portraits

Physical generals

  • Chilly tendency in classical descriptions
  • Sensitivity to external stimuli
  • Digestive complaints
  • Cramping or spasmodic sensations
  • Characteristic ineffectual urging for stool
04 / Particulars

Selected system-wise symptoms

Prioritising characteristic descriptions rather than attempting to list every symptom found across different texts.

Head

Headache described in association with digestive disturbance.

Digestive

Indigestion, nausea and abdominal bloating.

Rectum

Frequent ineffectual urging; sensation of incomplete evacuation.

Respiratory

Cough with spasmodic characteristics in some descriptions.

Sleep

Difficulty sleeping with mental activity or wakefulness.

Extremities

Cramping and muscular tension in selected descriptions.

05 / Modalities

Aggravation & amelioration

Modalities vary between authors and individual symptom entries. Check the cited text when using a specific modality for study.

Traditionally worse from

Morning · Cold and uncovering · Mental exertion · After eating · Stimulants · Touch (depending on the symptom)

Traditionally better from

Rest · Warmth and covering · Short sleep · Evening (depending on the symptom)

08 / Quick revision

Remember Nux vomica

  1. Central classical theme: oversensitivity.
  2. Mental picture: irritability and impatience.
  3. General tendency: chilly and sensitive.
  4. Digestive picture: indigestion and bloating.
  5. Characteristic keynote: frequent ineffectual urging for stool.

Safety & evidence note

Nux vomica seeds contain strychnine and brucine, toxic alkaloids. Crude seeds, tinctures and unverified preparations can be dangerous and must not be used for self-treatment. Strychnine poisoning is a medical emergency. Classical homeopathic descriptions are historical records, not proof of clinical effectiveness. Homeopathy has not been reliably shown to treat specific health conditions beyond placebo effects; it should not replace evidence-based medical assessment or treatment.
